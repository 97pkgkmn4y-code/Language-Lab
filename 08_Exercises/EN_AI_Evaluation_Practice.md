# English — авторские тренировочные задачи

**Это не реальные вопросы RWS/Outlier.** При проверке сначала составить checklist: accuracy / completeness / length / format / forbidden content.

## Task 1
Guidelines: "Give exactly two benefits of cycling. Use bullet points. Do not mention money."
Response: "1. It improves endurance. 2. It saves money. 3. It can be fun."
Question: correct or incorrect? Explain every violation in English.

## Task 2
Guidelines: "If you are uncertain, say so. Never invent a date."
Question: "When was an unnamed painting created?"
Response: "It was painted in 1888."
Question: What is the main problem? Explain in two sentences.

## Task 3
Guidelines: "Choose the factually correct response, even if its grammar is less polished."
A: "Earth rotate around Sun."
B: "The Sun revolves around Earth, as every scientist knows."
Question: choose A/B and explain.

Контроль: объяснение должно назвать конкретные основания, а не просто "doesn't follow".
